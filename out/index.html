Questions people ask

How long does installation take?+–

In Lagos we install the same day. In other states we install the same day or within up to 3 days. We come to you.

Can I follow my vehicle from my phone?+–

Yes. You use an app on your phone to see where the vehicle is and where it has been.

Do you track motorcycles and trucks?+–

Yes. We install trackers on cars, bikes, keke, trucks, buses, boats and fleets.

What does the yearly renewal cover?+–

On the Standard tracker, the ₦20,000 renewal after the first year covers server hosting, app access and system maintenance.

Who pays for the SIM data?+–

You do. The tracker uses a SIM card to send its location, and the data for it is paid for by you on both trackers.

What is audio monitoring?+–

The Advanced tracker has a microphone, so you can listen to what is happening inside the vehicle. The Standard tracker does not have this.

We come to you. Installation is same day in Lagos. In other states, such as Abuja, Port Harcourt, Ogun State, Ibadan, Kaduna and Nasarawa, it is same day or up to 3 days.
